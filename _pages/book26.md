# [Idea Glitch 1](https://amzn.eu/d/09VeBkZE)

<img src="/img/idea_glitch1.jpg" alt="Profile Picture" style="width: 150px; height: auto; border-radius: 50%;">

Taegang is an elite graduate of S University, one of South Korea’s most prestigious universities.

His life seemed perfect from the outside. Yet, for reasons no one could quite understand, Taegang suddenly quit the job he had been doing well in.

Now, in his hand, is a smartphone identical to the one used by his missing younger sister, Misol.

With nowhere else to turn, Taegang asks Hojae, a senior colleague from his former workplace, to help him find her.

But what they uncover is entangled in a chain of cause and effect neither of them could have imagined.

Will Taegang be able to find Misol?

A mystery novel about family—relationships that may feel close, yet are never easy to explain.
